# Code of Conduct

We want AI Studio to be a friendly, respectful place for everyone who uses or contributes to it.

## Our standard
- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers and different levels of experience.
- Respect other people's privacy: never post someone's keys, tokens, personal data or private media.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or sexual or violent content.
- Do not use the project to make media that imitates real people without their consent or that is meant to deceive or harm.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. Report a problem by opening a private security advisory on the repository, or by contacting the maintainer, Dinesh (dineshbalan001@gmail.com). Reports are handled privately.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
